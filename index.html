Getting started

Bring your plan here.

Your agent creates the plan. This workspace keeps your sessions and progress together.

  1. Open this page in a WebMCP-enabled agent browser.A regular browser can display your data, but cannot connect an agent without WebMCP support.
  2. Tell the agent your goals and constraints.Include your focus areas, equipment, available days, and session length.
  3. Ask it to save your program to Workout Planner.Your training will appear here when the agent writes the plan.

Already have a backup? Use Restore backup in settings.
